# FL-01 Setup Guide — Accounts, Course, and Project

This guide covers the physical setup steps you must complete yourself. Each step is tied to a deliverable and evaluation criterion.

---

## Step 1 — Create your free toolkit accounts

| Tool | URL | What to do | Evidence to capture |
|------|-----|-----------|---------------------|
| **Claude** | claude.ai | Sign up with your email (free tier works for Projects). | Profile/screenshot |
| **ChatGPT** | chat.openai.com | Sign up for a free account. | Profile/screenshot |
| **Anthropic Academy** | Use the AI Fluency link from the brief | Create an account (account required to enroll). | Account confirmation |

> **Note:** All three are free. The free tier of Claude supports Projects, which is all you need for this course.

---

## Step 2 — Enroll in the AI Fluency course

1. Log in to Anthropic Academy.
2. Find the **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** course.
3. Click **Enroll**.
4. Complete **at least the first module**.
5. Take a **screenshot** of your enrollment and/or completed module — this is evidence that the course was started.

---

## Step 3 — Create your Claude Project

1. Go to **claude.ai** → **Projects** → **Create project**.
2. Name it something like **"FL-01 Full Stack Learning"**.
3. Paste the custom instructions from `Claude_Project_Instructions.md`.
4. Optionally upload `FL-01_Workflow_Audit.md` as your knowledge base.
5. Save the project.
6. **Take a screenshot** of the configured project — this is your deliverable evidence.
